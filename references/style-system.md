# Visual system

The cover family is a tactile Chinese editorial collage, not a clean template.

## Shared traits

- warm cream paper, charcoal black, coral orange, mustard yellow;
- muted teal or purple only as a small grouping accent;
- torn fibers, curled edges, tape, layered shadows;
- photocopy grain, screen-print dots, ink scuffs, slight misregistration;
- oversized title with unequal line sizes and offsets;
- one heavy hand-painted arrow as a visual object;
- deliberate imbalance created by rotation, overlap, crop, and unequal scale.

## Composition rule

Stable does not mean aligned. Preserve a clear reading path while keeping the
physical objects irregular. At least three of these must be visible:

- unequal widths;
- alternating rotation;
- partial overlap;
- slight cropping at the frame edge;
- foreground object crossing two groups;
- noticeably different visual weight between left and right.

## Avoid

- clean grid or dashboard;
- equal-width, equal-height, equally spaced cards;
- centered symmetry;
- rounded UI panels;
- thin connector arrows;
- corporate PPT or conference backdrop;
- blue-purple neon, cyberpunk, robot, AI brain, chip, glassmorphism;
- dense small text that disappears on the profile grid.
