# Few-shot guide

The three bundled images are debranded examples. They are style and composition
references, not reusable copy.

## Selection

Use exactly one primary reference:

| Layout | Primary reference |
|---|---|
| process | `assets/examples/process-demo-debranded.png` |
| comparison | `assets/examples/comparison-demo-debranded.png` |
| evidence | `assets/examples/evidence-demo-debranded.png` |

Do not provide all three as equal image references. That averages the
composition into a generic poster.

## What to learn

- relative scale between title, visual objects, and conclusion;
- direction of the reading path;
- asymmetry, overlap, rotation, and partial crop;
- material texture and print imperfection;
- the weight of the large arrow.

## What not to copy

- any visible word, number, account name, logo, or watermark;
- exact topic objects;
- exact labels, percentages, or evidence;
- a specific title line or result.

When generating a background for deterministic text overlay, explicitly request
no readable text, no letters, and no numbers.
