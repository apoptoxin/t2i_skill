# Layout families

## Process

Use for a workflow, checklist, information cleanup, or messy-to-clear
transformation.

- quiet but jagged headline zone in the upper 34%;
- messy overlapping paper strips on the lower left;
- ordered but unequal ticket strips on the lower right;
- one oversized brush arrow crosses and overlaps both groups;
- tilted black result plaque near the lower right.

## Comparison

Use for old/new, before/after, wrong/right, or two competing approaches.

- title occupies roughly the upper 38%;
- old panel is narrower and leans slightly left;
- new panel is larger and visually lifted;
- panels use different materials and must not mirror each other;
- brush arrow moves diagonally from old to new and overlaps both;
- angled mustard conclusion strip at the bottom.

## Evidence

Use for three reports, sources, tests, or examples that converge on a result.

- title occupies roughly the upper 35%;
- three report sheets have different sizes and angles and overlap;
- three rough arrows converge into one large coral result plaque;
- magnifying glass overlaps data at lower left;
- tilted checklist scrap at lower right;
- irregular mustard summary strip at the bottom.
